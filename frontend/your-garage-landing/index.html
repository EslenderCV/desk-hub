OPEN HARDWARE DESIGN

Engineered for zero battery drain.

Traditional OBD dongles stay powered on and drain car batteries if left plugged in for days. Our custom ESP32 firmware monitors CAN bus voltage thresholds to enter true deep sleep when parked.

Deep Sleep & Auto-Wake

Draws 0.0mA while parked, waking instantly when alternator charging voltage exceeds 13.2V.

Offline Trip Buffering

Stores cumulative mileage increments locally on flash memory if your phone isn't connected.
